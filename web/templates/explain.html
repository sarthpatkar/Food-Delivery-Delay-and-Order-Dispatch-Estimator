3. K-Means Elbow Curve (Module 4 Practical 1)

Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (Inertia):

WCSS = Sum_{j=1}^{k} Sum_{x in S_j} ||x - mu_j||^2

The inflection point (elbow) mathematically confirms k = 3 as the optimal cluster partition.

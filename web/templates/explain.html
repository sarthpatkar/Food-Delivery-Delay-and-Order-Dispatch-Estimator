A* route search Module 2

f(n) = g(n) + h(n)

g is the cost of the road travelled so far and h is the straight-line Haversine distance to the drop-off.

cost = km * (m + r(m - 1))

m is the traffic multiplier for the road (Low 1.0, Medium 1.35, High 1.75, Jam 2.2) and r is the predicted delay probability.

h(n) ≤ true cost

Every road is at least as long as the straight line and the cost is never below km, so h is admissible and A* returns the cheapest route.
